This document contains extended results to complete the work published in [2]. All the pre-
sented results were produced by means of the SOS platform and source code for algorithms,
statistical analysis and generating tables are available in its online repository [1].


   Results are arranged as follows:
   • Tables 1, 2, 3 and Tables 4, 5, 6 display the comparison of DE/rand/1/exp, DE/rand/1/bin,
     and DE/current-to-rand/1 for non-rotated problems and for their rotated versions in
     10, 50 and 100 variables respectively;
   • Tables 7, 8, 9 and Tables 10, 11, 12 display the comparison of DE/rand/1/exp,
     DE/rand/1/bin, and DE/current-to-rand/1 for non-rotated problems and for their
     rotated versions in 10, 50 and 100 variables respectively;
   • Tables 13, 14, 15 and Tables 16, 17, 18 display the comparison of DE/rand/1/exp,
     DE/rand/1/bin, and DE/current-to-rand/1 for non-rotated problems and for their
     rotated versions in 10, 50 and 100 variables respectively;
   • Tables 19, 20, 21 and Tables 22, 23, 24 display the comparison of DE/rand/1/exp,
     DE/rand/1/bin, and DE/current-to-rand/1 for non-rotated problems and for their
     rotated versions in 10, 50 and 100 variables respectively;
   • Tables 25 and 26 display the results for the Holm-Bonferroni procedure of all the
     algorithms on non-rotated CEC2014 and rotated CEC2014 at 10, 50 and 100 dimension
     respectively;
   • Table 27 displays the comparisons between the aforementioned groups of algorithms
     on the first 2 real-world applications from CEC2011 [3];
   • Table 28 displays the results for the Holm-Bonferroni procedure of all the algorithms
     on Problem 1 and 2 of CEC2011 [3].
